A mechanism is only as strong as its weakest link. Each step shows its evidence type and status, so a chain that looks clean on paper reveals where it actually rests on one in-vitro run or a contested fetal sample.

Claims where the published literature and the data-derived models disagree, or where the literature disagrees with itself. This is the view PregBase does not have: every edge there carries equal weight.

Each card is one question from the notebook, scored against a shuffled-label baseline and a linear baseline, with the five-point trust audit and the known-truth check. Numbers are read straight from results_summary.json.

Demonstration knowledge base for the fetal / early-life immunity XAI project. Literature edges are drawn from published studies (Mishra 2021 Cell; Rackaityte 2020 Nat Med; de Goffau 2019 Nature; Kennedy 2023 Nature; Laursen 2021 Nat Microbiol; Henrick 2021 Cell; Frese 2017 mSphere). Model-derived edges come from the project notebook's own trust-audited results and should be read as hypotheses, not clinical facts. Not for clinical use.
